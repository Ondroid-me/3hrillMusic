// 3HRILL Music Studio

THE
SCIENCE
OF SOUND

Audio engineering is the invisible architecture of every record ever made. From Edison's tin-foil phonograph in 1877 to today's pocket-sized 32-bit float recorders, every technological leap has expanded what's possible — and lowered the barrier between an idea and a finished record.

At the core of all music production is one discipline: sound engineering. Understanding it means understanding how sound is captured, shaped, reproduced, and experienced — in a studio, at a festival, or on a laptop in a bedroom.

"The recording studio is an instrument. Learning to play it takes as long as learning to play any other."

— Brian Eno
